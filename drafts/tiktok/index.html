Turning TikTok visits into channel joins

Six drafts, each built around research-backed ideas. Every one keeps the TikTok escape coach, adds a 21+ check, and has a WHY THIS DRAFT? tab on the right edge with its evidence.

The drafts

Draft 1Hook

The first screen says what you sell, the price ("as low as $5/g"), the shipping cost and the proof, with one button. No more guessing what "Turn That Up" means.

57% of time on a page is spent on the first screen (NN/g). Extra costs are the #1 reason people quit a purchase (Baymard).

 Draft 2Proof first

Opens with your real chats, then a dark "Been burned before? So were they." section that answers the scam worry head-on.

5 reviews make a purchase ~270% more likely than none (Spiegel). 5 different buyers in your vouches mention scammers.

 Draft 3One tap

First screen goes from 9 things to tap down to 2. One big button stays at the bottom, where the thumb is, the whole visit.

More choices slow decisions (Hick's law). 6 jams sold 10× more often than 24 (Iyengar & Lepper).

 Draft 4Feed

Works like TikTok: one full screen per swipe, progress dots, button always at the bottom. The boldest change, so test it before betting on it.

People expect your site to work like the apps they already use (Jakob's law). People speed up near a goal (goal-gradient).

 Draft 5Lite

The same message at about 1/16 of the size. No web fonts, no animation frames, and the full menu with prices on screen one.

53% of mobile visits are dropped after 3 seconds (Google). 0.1 s faster = +8.4% retail conversions (Deloitte).

 Draft 6 · added Oct 27Proof + price

Proof first (Draft 2) with Hook's price headline on top: "Concentrates as low as $5/g", right above the real chats.

Price (anchoring) and proof (social proof) both on screen one, where 57% of attention goes.

My recommendation

Next homepage: Hook's first screen + One tap's bottom button + Proof's "Been burned before?" section. These are the safest bets. They change the first screen, which is where most attention goes, and they answer the worry your own buyers keep bringing up.

Apply Lite's lessons everywhere: smaller vouch thumbnails, and get the menu onto screen one or two.

Feed is the big swing. It could beat everything or annoy fast scrollers. Try it on its own for two weeks rather than mixing it in.

How to know it worked

Research shows what usually works. Only your own numbers can prove it for your buyers. The live site already sends button clicks to Google Analytics, so:

  1. Write down the last 2 weeks: visits from TikTok, and taps on Public Telegram channel.
  2. Put your bio link as turntupterpz.com/?src=tiktok so TikTok visits are easy to separate.
  3. Ship one change. Wait 2 weeks. Compare taps ÷ visits. Keep it if the number goes up, roll it back if not.
