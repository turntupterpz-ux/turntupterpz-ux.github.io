Getting people out of TikTok's browser

Four ways to tell TikTok visitors that Telegram buttons won't open in there, and how to fix it. Each one is the real homepage with one idea added.

Escape 1Coach

Show, don't tell. A looping mini phone acts out the exact taps, rings pulse on the real ••• button, and a hint chip points at it the whole visit.

 Escape 2Flip

Fix it where they tap. Telegram buttons wear a lock sticker. Tap one and it flips over into the two steps, with a dotted line drawn up to •••.

 Escape 3Gate

Tell them up front. A full-screen "hold up ✋" on arrival, then ordering reads as locked: a striped strip and "Unlock ordering" buttons.

 Escape 4Plan B

Give other ways out. A Telegram-style chat offers: open in Safari, copy the link, or copy @TurntUpTerp and search it in the Telegram app.

How to preview: open any draft on your phone. It pretends to be inside TikTok, with a fake TikTok bar at the top. Tap any Telegram button to see the idea. The fake ••• menu really works: "Open in browser" shows what happens after they escape. Use the DRAFT tab on the right edge to switch to Instagram, Android or normal Safari.
