About the products

Source and categories

What is TurntUpTerpz?

TurntUpTerpz is a small-batch, single-source menu focused on living-soil products and quality-first production. This website brings the current inventory, customer vouches, ordering options, shipping information, and direct contact choices into one mobile-friendly place.

What does small batch, living soil, and single source mean?

Small batch means products are made in limited runs with attention to each batch. Living soil refers to the cultivation approach used for the source material. Single source means the process stays under one source from cultivation through the finished product instead of combining material from unrelated suppliers.

What products are listed on the TurntUpTerpz menu?

The current menu may include live rosin, bubble hash, diamonds, cured badder, live resin, and melted-diamonds disposables. Strains, weights, hardware, prices, and stock status can change, so the menu on the homepage is the current reference.

What does 100% solventless mean on the menu?

On this menu, 100% solventless identifies concentrate categories produced without hydrocarbon solvents during extraction. Live rosin and bubble hash are labeled solventless when applicable.

What do H, I-H, S-H, and S mean?

The menu uses H for Hybrid, I-H for Indica Hybrid, S-H for Sativa Hybrid, and S for Sativa. These labels describe the listed classification of each strain.

Current availability

Menu and pricing

How do I check current inventory and pricing?

Go to the TurntUpTerpz homepage and select View current menu. The Inventory tab shows the menu date, categories, available strains, weights, prices, hardware, and low-stock notes. Use the dated website menu rather than an older screenshot.

Why can inventory or pricing change?

Small-batch availability is limited, and strains or hardware can sell through at different times. The menu date and low-stock labels help identify the latest published information. Confirm current details before completing an order.

Ways to connect

Ordering and verification

Can I order and pay through the Telegram bot?

Yes. TurntUpStoreBot is the self-service option for browsing, ordering, and making payment without first sending a personal message. Review the order details carefully before completing payment.

How can I ask a question or request FaceTime verification?

Questions, concerns, and FaceTime verification requests can be sent through personal Telegram or by iMessage and SMS. The Telegram bot remains available separately for self-service orders.

Why does the iMessage or SMS button not work inside TikTok?

TikTok’s in-app browser may not open sms: links. Tap the three dots in the top-right corner, choose Open in browser, and then use the Text or iMessage button from your regular browser.

Before placing an order

Shipping information

Where can I find current shipping information?

Open View current menu on the homepage and select Shipping info. Shipping details can change, so review the current page and ask directly if your situation is not covered before placing an order.

Am I responsible for checking the laws in my location?

Yes. This website is intended only for adults who are legally permitted to view and obtain the listed products in their location. Visitors are responsible for understanding and following applicable local laws. This website does not provide legal or medical advice.

Customer experience

Vouches and direct contact

Where can I see customer vouches?

The homepage vouch gallery contains 51 customer conversation screenshots. Tap any vouch for full-screen viewing, then swipe or use the arrows to continue. Personal details are obscured in the published screenshots.

Can I contact a person instead of using the bot?

Yes. The bot is available for self-service ordering, while personal Telegram and iMessage or SMS remain available for questions, concerns, and verification requests.
