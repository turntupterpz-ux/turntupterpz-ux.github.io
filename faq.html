About the products

What is TurntUpTerpz?

TurntUpTerpz is a small-batch, single-source concentrate producer based in California's Emerald Triangle. Current stock, customer vouches, ordering, shipping information, and direct contact are all available through this site and Telegram.

What does small batch, living soil, and single source mean?

Small batch means products are made in limited runs with attention to each batch. Living soil refers to the cultivation approach used for the source material. Single source means the process stays under one source from cultivation through the finished product instead of combining material from unrelated suppliers.

Do you carry top-shelf and budget options?

Yes. Stock generally spans both top-shelf, solventless categories and more budget-friendly options, so there's a price point for most customers. Current strains, weights, and pricing are posted in the Telegram channel.

What do H, I-H, S-H, and S mean?

H stands for Hybrid, I-H for Indica Hybrid, S-H for Sativa Hybrid, and S for Sativa. These labels describe a strain's classification when they're listed.

Ordering & verification

How do I see current stock and pricing?

Tap Inventory / Order Now on the homepage. It opens the Telegram channel where current stock, weights, and pricing are posted and kept up to date.

How do I place an order?

Start with Inventory / Order Now to see what's currently available, then message directly on Telegram or by iMessage, email, or SMS to arrange your order.

Is FaceTime verification available?

Yes. Scams are common in this space, so FaceTime verification is available on request to show current stock before you order. Message directly on Telegram to request it.

How do I know I'm dealing with a legitimate seller?

Look for FaceTime verification, real customer vouches, and a willingness to answer direct questions before you pay. TurntUpTerpz offers all three: request FaceTime verification, browse the vouch gallery on the homepage, and message directly with any questions or concerns.

Why does the iMessage or email button not work inside TikTok?

TikTok's and Instagram's in-app browsers can block Telegram and sms: links from opening correctly. Tap the three dots in the top-right corner, choose Open in browser, and then use the button from your regular browser.

Shipping & insurance

What are the shipping options and costs?

Same-day shipping is available 6 days a week, Monday through Saturday, for orders placed before 2PM PST. Standard domestic shipping is $15 and takes 2–5 business days. Overnight domestic shipping is $50. Worldwide shipping is $25–$35 and takes 7–21 days, with expedited options available.

Is shipping insured?

Yes, every order is insured, domestic and international. For domestic orders, if tracking never shows delivered, the order is reshipped at no cost. For international orders, that reship is covered at 50% at no cost to you.

Am I responsible for checking the laws in my location?

Yes. This website is intended only for adults who are legally permitted to view and obtain the listed products in their location. Visitors are responsible for understanding and following applicable local laws. This website does not provide legal or medical advice.
