About the products

  • What is TurntUpTerpz?

    A small-batch, single-source concentrate producer based in California's Emerald Triangle. Current stock, vouches, ordering, shipping info and direct contact are all here and on Telegram.

  • What do small batch, living soil and single source mean?

    Small batch: limited runs with attention to each batch. Living soil: the way the source material is grown. Single source: one source from cultivation to finished product, never mixed with material from unrelated suppliers.

  • Do you carry top-shelf and budget options?

    Yes. Stock spans top-shelf solventless and budget-friendly options. Current strains, weights and prices are posted in the Telegram folder.

  • What do H, I-H, S-H, I and S mean?

    Hybrid, indica hybrid, sativa hybrid, indica and sativa — the strain's classification.

Shipping & insurance

  • What are the shipping options and costs?
